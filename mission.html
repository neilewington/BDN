Our Mission

Helping people build a real life in Barcelona

Barcelona Digital Nomads exists to turn a city full of strangers into a community of people who actually show up for each other.

Moving to a new city is easy to do and hard to do well. Anyone can land in Barcelona — the harder part is finding your people, building a routine, and turning a temporary stay into somewhere that feels like home.

That's what we're here for. Since 2018, BDN has grown from a handful of nomads meeting for coffee into one of the biggest communities of digital nomads, founders, and entrepreneurs in the city — bringing people together in person every week, and online every day.

We believe community is built in the room, not just online. So while we run an active online community, everything we do is designed to get people off their laptops and into the same physical space — meetups, workshops, bar nights, and events that turn acquaintances into actual friends and collaborators.
